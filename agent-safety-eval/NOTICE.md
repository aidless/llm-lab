# Notice and evidence boundary

This repository contains an original, synthetic, mock-only Agent Safety
evaluation harness. It does not redistribute task text or data from AgentDojo,
InjecAgent, AgentHarm, Agent-SafetyBench, ToolEmu, SafeArena, or Concordia.

## What the package demonstrates

- frozen task and judge-case hashes;
- a deterministic four-arm intervention ablation (`off`, `prompt`, `gate`,
  `on`) at the tool boundary;
- complete per-step trajectory records;
- explicit model, parse, tool, and policy outcomes;
- reproducible JSON, Markdown, and HTML reports;
- separate assistant-draft and human-review label provenance;
- an independent evidence verifier and SHA-256 manifest.

## What it does not demonstrate

- production guardrail effectiveness;
- real filesystem, shell, email, or network safety;
- a frontier-model safety ranking;
- a new benchmark taxonomy or benchmark leadership;
- regulatory compliance or field safety;
- that assistant-authored draft labels are human gold labels.

All tool execution is against an in-memory mock environment. The local models
are small CPU models served by Ollama. Any observed differences are scoped to
these frozen tasks, model digests, and policy implementation.

## Dataset provenance

`dataset/tasks-v1.jsonl` and `dataset/judge-cases-v1.jsonl` are generated by
`agent_safety_eval.dataset_builder`. Their hashes are recorded in
`dataset/manifest.json`. Public agent-safety projects informed the design
space, but no upstream rows are copied.
